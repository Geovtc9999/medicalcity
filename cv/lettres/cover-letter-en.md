## Terminology to keep consistent in English

Use **reconciliation**, **breaks** (not "gaps" alone), **completeness**, **retention**, **legal hold**, **retrievability**, **journalling**, **entitlements**, **supervised population**, **joiners / movers / leavers**, **first-line control**, **segregation of duties**, **tamper-evident**, **evidence pack**. Avoid translating "recette" as "recipe" — it is **testing** or **UAT** — and avoid "control" in the French sense of *vérification* when you mean **check** or **control** in the compliance sense; in banking English, *control* is the right word, so say "automated first-line control", never "automatic verification".
